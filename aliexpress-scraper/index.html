What does AliExpress Scraper do?

AliExpress Scraper collects public product data from AliExpress search. Give it keywords or search URLs, and it returns one clean record per product: product ID, URL, title, price and currency, original price, discount, star rating, order count and images. Turn on reviews and each product also gets its buyer reviews with star rating, English text, original text, date, buyer country, purchased variant and photos. Reviewer names are never collected. You choose the ship-to country, currency and language, so prices match the market you sell in. A search page returns 60 products in one request, so runs are fast and cheap. It is built for dropshippers, ecommerce sellers, price trackers and market researchers. It costs $4 per 1,000 products, and failed items are not charged. For variants, specifications and store details, use AliExpress Product Details Scraper.

FAQ

Do I need an AliExpress account or cookies?

No. The actor only reads what a logged-out visitor sees. It never asks for or uses login cookies.

How do I get variants, specifications and store details?

Use AliExpress Product Details Scraper. Pass it the product URLs from this actor's results. Product URLs given to this actor are listed in FAILED_ITEMS with that pointer and are not charged.

How do I get prices in my currency and for my country?

Set shipTo, currency and language, for example DE, EUR and de_DE. AliExpress then returns prices and availability for that market.

Why did something end up in FAILED_ITEMS?

AliExpress sometimes shows a verification page instead of data. The actor never solves verification pages. It retries on a new residential IP, and if every attempt is blocked it skips the item without charging you.

Which proxy should I use?

Residential, which is the default. Datacenter IPs are blocked often.

Does it collect personal data?

Reviews include rating, text, date, buyer country and photos. Reviewer names and profile pictures are left out.
