What does LinkedIn Ad Library Scraper do?

LinkedIn Ad Library Scraper collects ads from the public LinkedIn Ad Library, the transparency site where LinkedIn lists ads that ran on its platform. Search by advertiser name, by keyword in the ad text, or paste Ad Library search and ad URLs. You can limit searches to countries and to a date range. For every ad you get the advertiser and its LinkedIn page, who paid for the ad, the ad format, the full ad text, headline, call to action, landing URL and image, video or carousel media URLs. When LinkedIn shows them, you also get the run dates, the total impressions range, the share of impressions per country and the targeting the advertiser picked (language, location and which audience parameters were used). It works without a LinkedIn account, cookies or login. Use it for competitor ad research, creative inspiration, B2B market research and lead lists of companies that advertise on LinkedIn.

FAQ

Do I need a LinkedIn account?

No. The LinkedIn Ad Library is public and the scraper never logs in or uses cookies. If LinkedIn answers with a login wall or a challenge, the scraper retries on a new IP and never tries to get past it.

Which countries are covered?

Every country in the Ad Library country filter. Ads can be searched worldwide or limited with the countries input. LinkedIn shows run dates, impressions, country shares and targeting for some ads only. In our tests they appeared on ads served in Europe, while ads without them still came with advertiser, payer, format, text and media.

How far back does it go?

As far as the Ad Library goes. The custom date filter on the site accepts dates up to one year back. Searches without a date range return what LinkedIn lists, newest first.

Can I see ad spend?

No. LinkedIn does not publish spend in the Ad Library. You get the total impressions range and the share of impressions per country where LinkedIn shows them.
