What does Instagram Post Scraper do?

Scrape posts from public Instagram profiles, or single posts and reels by URL. No Instagram login is needed: the actor reads the same data Instagram shows to logged-out visitors, through residential proxies.

For each post:

  • id, shortCode, url, type (Image, Video or Sidecar), productType
  • caption, hashtags, mentions, alt
  • likesCount, commentsCount, timestamp
  • displayUrl, images, dimensionsHeight, dimensionsWidth
  • videoUrl and videoDuration for videos, musicInfo for reels
  • childPosts for carousels
  • ownerUsername, ownerFullName, ownerId
  • latestComments and firstComment: the first comments Instagram shows on the post, with text, author, likes and date

Profiles are paginated 12 posts at a time, so resultsLimit works beyond the first page. In local tests a profile returned posts from the second page without cookies.

Limits without cookies:

  • videoViewCount and videoPlayCount are null. Instagram does not show view counts on single posts to logged-out visitors. The Instagram Reels Scraper does return play counts.
  • Comments come from the first batch Instagram shows, about 15 per post, so maxComments above 15 has no extra effect.
  • Pinned posts cannot be detected, so skipPinnedPosts only works with cookies. Pinned posts show up first and can be older than the rest.
  • isSponsored is always false because the paid partnership flag is not shown to logged-out visitors.
  • Private profiles and posts hidden from logged-out visitors (for example age-restricted posts) are skipped and listed in FAILED_ITEMS.
  • Every post costs one request to load its details, so large limits take longer.
