What does YouTube Transcript Scraper do?

Get the full transcript of any YouTube video or Short, with timestamps, in the original language or translated into any language YouTube supports. Each result also includes the video's title, channel, views, likes, description, thumbnails and publish date.

You only pay for videos that return a transcript. Videos that are removed, private, have no captions, or fail after all retries are skipped and not charged. They are listed in FAILED_VIDEOS in the run's key-value store with the reason.

  • Feed transcripts to ChatGPT, Claude or your own LLM for summaries, Q&A and RAG
  • Turn videos into blog posts, newsletters and social posts
  • Research competitors, podcasts, lectures and interviews at scale
  • Build subtitle, search or translation datasets

FAQ

A video was skipped. Was I charged?

No. Only results written to the dataset are charged. Check FAILED_VIDEOS for the reason.

The video has no captions at all. It is skipped. YouTube has no transcript to return. Can I use it from an AI agent?

Yes. It works through the Apify MCP server, and transcript_only_text is ready to pass to a model.
