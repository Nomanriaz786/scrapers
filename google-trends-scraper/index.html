What does Google Trends Scraper do?

Get Google Trends data as clean JSON without opening the Trends website. Look up one keyword or compare up to 5 keywords on the same scale, for any country or worldwide, and get interest over time, interest by region and related queries (top and rising). Optionally add the current trending searches for a country.

You only pay for results that are returned. A term with too little search volume, a request Google rejects, or a request still failing after all retries is skipped and not charged. Skipped requests are listed in FAILED_ITEMS in the run's key-value store with the reason.

  • Keyword research and SEO: compare keywords and see which one people search for more
  • Seasonality: see when interest in a product or topic peaks during the year
  • Local targeting: find the countries or states where a keyword is most popular
  • Content ideas: collect related and rising queries around a topic
  • News and social monitoring: pull the daily trending searches for a country
