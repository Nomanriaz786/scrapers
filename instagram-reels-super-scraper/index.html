What does Instagram Reels Scraper do?

Instagram Reels Scraper collects reels from public Instagram profiles, and single reels from their URLs, without an Instagram login. For each reel it returns the play count, like and comment counts, caption, hashtags, mentions, publish date, duration, video and cover URLs, audio artist and title, tagged users, collaborators, the owner's username and name, and the first comments Instagram shows. It reads a profile's reels tab, which is where Instagram shows play counts to logged-out visitors, pages through it 12 reels at a time up to your limit, and can skip pinned reels. Requests go through rotating residential proxies with retries on a new IP, and only complete reels are pushed, so you never pay for empty rows. Results export to JSON, CSV or Excel, or come back through the Apify API and MCP server. It is built for short-form video research, creator analytics, trend tracking and competitor monitoring.

FAQ

Do I need an Instagram account or cookies?

No. Play counts, likes, comments and video URLs all come back without a login. Cookies from your own secondary account are optional.

Why do some reels have no play count?

Play counts are shown only in a profile's reels tab. Reels added by direct URL have videoPlayCount set to null, so scrape the profile if you need plays.

Can I skip pinned reels?

Yes. Turn on skipPinnedPosts; it works without cookies because the reels tab marks pinned reels.

Can I download the videos?

Each reel includes videoUrl, a direct link to the video file. Instagram signs these links, so they expire after a while; download them soon after the run.

What am I charged for?

Each reel pushed to the dataset is one result. Failed inputs and skipped reels are not charged. See the Pricing tab for the current price.
