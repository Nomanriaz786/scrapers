What does LinkedIn Jobs Scraper do?

LinkedIn Jobs Scraper collects public job postings from LinkedIn without a login, cookies or a LinkedIn account. You give it job keywords and a location, LinkedIn search URLs copied from your browser, or individual job URLs. In one run it pages through the search results and then opens every job page, so each result already contains the full description (plain text and HTML), salary range when the employer shows one, applicant count, seniority level, employment type, industries, company name, company page and logo, location, posting date and whether the job still accepts applications. Duplicate jobs across your queries are removed automatically. You can narrow searches by date posted, from the last few hours up to the last month. Results download as JSON, CSV, Excel or HTML, or flow into Google Sheets, Make, Zapier and your own code through the Apify API. You pay only for jobs delivered.

FAQ

Do I need a LinkedIn account?

No. The scraper reads the same public job pages LinkedIn shows to logged-out visitors. It never logs in, never uses cookies or accounts, and never tries to get past a login wall or a captcha. If LinkedIn blocks a request, the scraper retries from a new IP and otherwise skips the job.

Why do I get fewer jobs than LinkedIn shows?

LinkedIn shows logged-out visitors fewer results than members see and stops serving a search after roughly 1,000 results. The count LinkedIn displays is also an estimate that includes promoted and duplicate listings. The scraper removes duplicates across all your queries, so the same job is never returned or charged twice. To collect more, split a broad search into several narrower ones by city or keyword.

Which filters work?

Keywords, location (as text, or geoId from a pasted search URL) and date posted (postedWithin: last N hours, 24 hours, week or month). We tested the other LinkedIn filters on 2026-10-17 with logged-out requests: workplace type (remote, hybrid, on-site), experience level, job type and sort order returned exactly the same jobs as without the filter. LinkedIn only applies them for logged-in members, so this scraper does not offer them and ignores them in pasted search URLs rather than pretend they work. If LinkedIn writes "(Remote)" or "(Hybrid)" in a job's location, the scraper fills workplaceType from it.

Is it legal to scrape LinkedIn jobs?

This scraper collects only job postings that LinkedIn publishes to anyone on the web without a login. It does not collect member profiles or any data behind a login. Note that LinkedIn's User Agreement prohibits scraping, and laws on web scraping and on processing personal data, such as GDPR, differ by country. You are responsible for how you use the scraper and the data, including complying with LinkedIn's terms and the laws that apply to you. This is not legal advice; if in doubt, ask a lawyer.
