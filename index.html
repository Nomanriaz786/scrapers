Web scrapers for YouTube, LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, AliExpress and more

14 ready-to-run scrapers on Apify. No code needed, pay only for results, export to JSON, CSV or Excel, or call them from your app and AI agents.

  • AliExpress Scraper

    Scrape AliExpress search results and buyer reviews: title, price, discount, rating, orders, images and reviews with photos. No login. Pay only for products returned. Set ship-to country and currency.

  • Bilibili Scraper

    Scrape Bilibili videos by URL, user or search term: title, stats, tags, author, plus optional comments and danmaku. No login or cookies needed. Pay only for videos returned.

  • China Supplier Scraper

    Search Made-in-China, Yiwugo and Global Sources by keyword or URL. Get product prices, MOQ, images, specs and supplier name, location, business type, years and badges. No login. Pay only for results.

  • Google Trends Scraper

    Get Google Trends data for up to 5 compared keywords: interest over time, interest by region, related queries and topics, plus daily trending searches by country. Pay only for results returned.

  • Instagram Comments Scraper

    Scrape comments from public Instagram posts and reels: text, author, likes, time. Works without login for about 15 comments per post; add your own cookies to request more comments and replies.

  • Instagram Hashtag Scraper

    Scrape Instagram posts or reels for any hashtag: caption, author, likes, comment count, images, time and optional latest comments. Requires the cookies of your own Instagram account.

  • Instagram Post Scraper

    Caption, likes, comment count, date, images, video URL, hashtags, mentions and top comments from public Instagram profiles and post URLs. No login needed. Date filter and per-profile limit included.

  • Instagram Profile Scraper

    Name, bio, followers, following, post count, bio links, verified status, latest 12 posts and related profiles from public Instagram profiles. No login needed. Optionally add your own cookies for exact counts.

  • Instagram Reels Scraper

    Plays, likes, comment count, caption, date, duration, video URL, audio and top comments from public Instagram profiles and reel URLs. No login needed. Skip pinned reels and set a limit per profile.

  • TikTok Video Scraper

    Scrape TikTok video data from URLs: text, author, followers, plays, likes, comments, shares, saves, hashtags, music and subtitles. Optional MP4, cover and slideshow downloads. No login or cookies needed.

  • Weibo Scraper

    Scrape Weibo hot search and public posts by keyword, topic or account: text, time, reposts, comments, likes, images, video and topics. No login or cookies needed. Pay only for results returned.

  • YouTube Comments Scraper

    Scrape YouTube comments from any video URL: comment text, author, likes, reply count, publish time, creator heart and channel owner flags. Sort by top or newest. No API key or quota.

  • YouTube Scraper

    Get YouTube video titles, views, likes, comment counts, upload dates, durations, descriptions and channel stats from search terms, channel URLs (videos, Shorts, live) or video URLs. Export as JSON, CSV or Excel.

  • YouTube Transcript Scraper

    Get timestamped transcripts from any YouTube video or Short, in the spoken language or auto-translated into any language, plus title, channel, views and likes. Pay only for videos that return a transcript.
