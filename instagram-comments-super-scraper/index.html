What does Instagram Comments Scraper do?

Get the comments of public Instagram posts and reels: comment text, author username and ID, profile picture, likes, reply count and time posted.

Do you need cookies? No, not to start.

  • Without cookies you get the first page of top-level comments that Instagram shows to logged-out visitors, which is about 15 comments per post. Replies are not included.
  • With cookies from your own Instagram account the scraper can page through more comments and fetch replies (up to your limits).

FAQ

Do I need cookies?

No. Without cookies you get about 15 top-level comments per post and no replies. Add cookies when you need more comments or replies.

Why did my run fail with expired cookies?

The message "Your Instagram cookies expired or were rejected; refresh them" means Instagram answered with a login page, a 401, login_required or checkpoint_required. The session was logged out, expired or needs a security check. Log in to the account in your browser, clear any check Instagram shows, then copy fresh cookies. If you gave several sessions, the rejected one is dropped and the others keep working.

Is my account safe?

Use a secondary account. Instagram can rate-limit or challenge any account that loads many pages quickly, and that is outside this scraper's control. The cookies field is stored encrypted and its values are never logged.

How many comments can I get?

Without cookies, about 15 per post: the first page Instagram shows to logged-out visitors. With cookies, up to resultsLimit per post (0 means no limit), plus up to maxRepliesPerComment replies per comment.
