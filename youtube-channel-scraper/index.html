What does YouTube Channel Scraper do?

YouTube Channel Scraper returns one clean record per YouTube channel with the numbers and profile details shown on the channel page and in its About panel. Give it channel URLs in any format (@handle, /channel/UC..., /c/name or /user/name), or give it keywords and it finds matching channels through YouTube search with the channel filter. For each channel you get the channel ID, handle, title, full description, subscriber count, exact video count, exact total views, country, joined date, avatar, banner, verified status, channel keywords and every external link from the About panel, with YouTube's redirect wrapper removed so you get the real website and social profile URLs. Turn on latest videos to add the newest uploads with views, published time and duration. It needs no YouTube API key, no login and no cookies. You are only charged for channels that come back complete with an ID and a title.

FAQ

Do I need a YouTube API key or an account?

No. The scraper reads the same public data YouTube shows to a logged-out visitor. There is no quota to manage and nothing to log in to.

Which channel URL formats work?

youtube.com/@handle, youtube.com/channel/UC..., youtube.com/c/name and youtube.com/user/name. Extra path parts such as /videos or /about are fine. URLs on other hosts are refused and listed in FAILED_ITEMS.

Why is the subscriber count rounded?

YouTube only publishes a rounded subscriber figure (for example 21.4M) to everyone except the channel owner. The scraper turns it into a number (21400000). Video count and total views are exact.

Does it return the channel's business email?

No. YouTube hides the business email behind a sign-in and a captcha. Emails written in the description text are returned as part of description.

What happens when a channel cannot be scraped?

It is retried on a fresh proxy IP up to Max request retries times. Channels that do not exist, URLs that are not channels and channels that still fail are skipped, not charged, and listed with the reason in the FAILED_ITEMS record of the run's key-value store.
