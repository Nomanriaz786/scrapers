What does AliExpress Product Details Scraper do?

AliExpress Product Details Scraper opens AliExpress product pages and returns one complete record per product. Give it product URLs and it returns the title, price range and currency, original price, discount, rating, order count, all images, the store (name, URL, positive feedback rating, followers), the specification table, and every variant (SKU) with its option names, image, price, original price, stock and availability. Turn on reviews to add buyer reviews with rating, English text, original text, date, country, purchased variant and photos; reviewer names are never collected. Set the ship-to country, currency and language to see prices for your market. AliExpress protects this data more heavily than search, so some products can be skipped after retries; skipped products are never charged. It costs $5 per 1,000 products. For fast search results, use AliExpress Scraper and feed its product URLs here.

FAQ

Do I need an AliExpress account or cookies?

No. It reads only what a logged-out visitor sees and never uses login cookies.

Why were some products skipped?

AliExpress often answers product-detail requests with a slider check. This actor never solves those. It retries on new residential IPs, and if every attempt is blocked the product goes to FAILED_ITEMS and is not charged. In a test on residential proxies, 6 of 10 products came back on the first run. Running the skipped URLs again later usually recovers more of them.

How do I get product URLs in bulk?

Run AliExpress Scraper for your keywords and pass the url field of its results to this actor.

How do I get prices for my country and currency?

Set shipTo, currency and language, for example DE, EUR and de_DE.

Does it collect personal data?

Reviews include rating, text, date, buyer country and photos. Reviewer names and profile pictures are left out.
