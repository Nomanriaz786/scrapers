What does YouTube Channel Contact Scraper do?

YouTube Channel Contact Scraper finds the business contact details that YouTube creators publish openly. Give it channel URLs or search keywords and it returns, for each channel, the emails written in the channel description, the website and social links from the About section, and emails listed on the creator's own website. Every email comes with the page it was found on, so you can check the source yourself. It also returns channel stats such as subscribers, videos, total views, country and join date. The scraper only reads public pages. It never logs in, never uses cookies and never touches YouTube's hidden "View email address" button, which requires a signed-in account and a captcha. It does not guess or generate emails. Agencies and brands use it to build influencer outreach lists. You pay $50 per 1,000 channels, and only for channels where an email was found.

FAQ

Why are some channels missing an email?

Many creators only offer their email through YouTube's "View email address" button, which needs a signed-in account and a captcha. This scraper does not use that button, so those emails are not returned. Others list only social profiles or a contact form. In a test on 50 channels from keyword searches, about 1 in 4 had a public email. By default only those are returned; turn on includeChannelsWithoutEmail to also get channels with just a website or social links.

Is this legal and GDPR compliant?

The scraper only collects contact details that creators chose to publish for business enquiries. Whether you may use them depends on your use and your country. Under GDPR and similar laws, business emails of individuals are still personal data, so you need a lawful basis such as legitimate interest, you must tell people where you got their data and you must honor opt-outs. This is not legal advice.

How are results charged?

$50 per 1,000 channels returned, which is $0.05 per channel. By default a channel is only returned, and only charged, when at least one public email was found, so you never pay for a channel without an email. If you turn on includeChannelsWithoutEmail, channels with only a website or social links are returned and charged too. Searched channels with no contact and failed inputs are never charged.

Can it find hidden YouTube emails?

No. The email behind YouTube's "View email address" button is not collected. Getting it needs a logged-in account and solving a captcha, and this scraper does neither.

Does it work with handles and old channel URLs?

Yes. /@handle, /channel/UC..., /c/name and /user/name all work. Duplicate channels are returned once.
