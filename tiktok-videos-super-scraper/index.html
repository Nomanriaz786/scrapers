What does TikTok Video Scraper do?

TikTok Video Scraper takes a list of public TikTok video URLs and returns one structured result per video. Each result holds the caption text, language, creation date, country of upload, and the counts for plays, likes, comments, shares and saves. It also includes the author's username, nickname, verification badge, follower and following counts, total likes and video count, plus the music title, artist and audio link. Hashtags and mentioned accounts come out as clean lists, and subtitle tracks are listed with their language. If you tick the download options, the actor saves the MP4 video, the cover image, slideshow photos and WebVTT subtitles in the run's key-value store and links them in the result. It reads the same public page that a logged-out visitor sees, so you never share a TikTok account. Deleted or private videos are skipped and not charged.

FAQ

What happens with private or deleted videos?

The actor skips them. TikTok reports these videos as unavailable, so there is no data to return. Each one is listed in FAILED_ITEMS with the reason, for example Video unavailable (10204: item doesn't exist).

Are failed videos charged?

No. A result is saved and charged only when it has a real video ID, author and creation time. Skipped and failed videos cost nothing.

Do download links expire?

Files you download with the actor are stored in your Apify key-value store and stay there for as long as your storage retention allows. The TikTok links inside the result, such as playUrl, coverUrl and subtitle downloadLink, are signed by TikTok and expire, in our tests after about two days. Tick the download options if you need lasting copies.

Do I need cookies or a TikTok account?

No. The actor reads public video pages the way a logged-out visitor sees them. You do not need to log in or provide cookies.
