What does Instagram Profile Scraper do?

Get public profile data from Instagram profiles by username or profile URL. No Instagram login is needed: the actor reads the same data Instagram shows to logged-out visitors, through residential proxies.

For each public profile:

  • id, username, fullName, biography, url
  • followersCount and followsCount (exact)
  • postsCount, rounded the way Instagram shows it to visitors (for example 32000 for "32K Posts")
  • externalUrl, externalUrlShimmed and all bio links in externalUrls
  • verified, private, profilePicUrl
  • latestPosts: the latest 12 posts with id, shortcode, type, caption, hashtags, mentions, image URL and alt text
  • relatedProfiles: the accounts Instagram suggests next to the profile, up to about 50

Limits without cookies:

  • likesCount, commentsCount and timestamp in latestPosts are null, because the logged-out profile grid does not include them. Use the Instagram Post Scraper if you need engagement per post.
  • profilePicUrlHD is the same 150 px picture as profilePicUrl.
  • businessCategoryName, isBusinessAccount, highlightReelCount and igtvVideoCount are not shown to logged-out visitors and come back empty or 0.
  • Posts of private profiles cannot be scraped, unless the account behind your cookies follows them.
  • Numeric user IDs are not supported. Use usernames or profile URLs.
