What does Bilibili Scraper do?

Scrape Bilibili (哔哩哔哩) videos without login or cookies. Give it video URLs, user space URLs or search keywords, and get one clean record per video: title, description, author, publish date, duration, views, likes, coins, favorites, shares, danmaku count, comment count, tags and thumbnail. Optionally add top comments and the full danmaku (bullet comment) feed with timestamps.

You only pay for videos that are returned. Videos that are removed, private, region-locked or still failing after all retries are skipped and not charged. They are listed in FAILED_ITEMS in the run's key-value store with the reason.

  • Research trends on Bilibili by keyword
  • Collect danmaku for audience sentiment and engagement analysis by timestamp
  • Monitor a creator's newest uploads
  • Build Chinese video datasets for research or LLM work

FAQ

Do I need a Bilibili account?

No. Everything comes from public endpoints that work for logged-out visitors.

Why did some videos fail?

Removed, private or region-locked videos cannot be scraped. Check FAILED_ITEMS in the key-value store. You are not charged for them.

Which proxy should I use?

Bilibili rate-limits busy IPs. Residential proxies with a low maxConcurrency give the most stable runs.
