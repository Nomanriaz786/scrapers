What does China Supplier Scraper do?

China Supplier Scraper searches three of the largest Chinese wholesale and B2B marketplaces, Made-in-China.com, Yiwugo and Global Sources, and returns one clean row per product. Type a keyword such as "led strip" and pick the sites you want, or paste product and supplier URLs. For every product you get the title, link, price or price range, currency, minimum order quantity (MOQ) with its unit, images and any specifications shown on the listing. Each row also carries the supplier's name, page, location, business type, years on the platform and badges such as Audited Supplier, Diamond Member or Verified Manufacturer. A source field tells you which site the product came from, so you can compare offers side by side. It is built for sourcing agents, dropshippers, importers and buyers who want a shortlist of suppliers without copying pages by hand. No login, account or cookies are needed.

FAQ

How is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) reported?

As a number in minOrder and the unit the supplier uses in minOrderUnit, for example 1000 and Meters. When a product has quantity price tiers, the MOQ is the start of the first tier and priceText lists every tier price.

How do I contact the suppliers?

Each product has the supplier's page in supplier.url. Use the site's own inquiry or chat button there. The actor returns a company address only where a site publishes it as company information. It does not collect names, phone numbers, e-mail addresses or messenger IDs of individual people, even when a page shows them.

Which sites are supported?

Made-in-China.com, Yiwugo (en.yiwugo.com, the Yiwu International Trade City catalogue) and Global Sources (globalsources.com). Choose any of them with the sites input.

Which URLs can I paste?

Product pages from all three sites, Made-in-China supplier showrooms and Yiwugo shop pages. Global Sources supplier homepages are not supported yet; search by keyword or use their product URLs instead.

Do I need an account or cookies?

No. The actor only reads public pages and never logs in.

Which proxy should I use?

The default Apify datacenter proxy, which is the cheapest option. If a site starts returning blocks, switch the proxy group to residential.

Does it bypass captchas?

No. If a site answers with a captcha or verification page, the page is retried on a new IP and, if that keeps happening, skipped and not charged.
